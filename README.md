# complex_election
A modeling project that demonstrates how electoral systems shape ideological representation and the viability and persistence of extremist party:

Proportional Representation is more permissive to a formation of new parties that lead to better representation than First-Past-The-Post but is also more vulnerable to invasion from an extremist party.

However, once extremist party gain foothold in First-Past-The-Post, they also become harder to dislodge by challengers.
